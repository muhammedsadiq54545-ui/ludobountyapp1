01 Overview & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to Ludo Bounty (Package ID: com.ludobounty.game). Your privacy is of fundamental importance to us. We believe in transparency and providing a clean, fun, and safe classic board game experience for players worldwide.

Zero Sensitive Data Principle: Ludo Bounty does not require user registration, account sign-ups, phone numbers, contact lists, GPS location, microphones, or camera access. You can play the full game completely offline or locally without transmitting personal identities to external servers.

02 Information We Collect and Process

Ludo Bounty is primarily designed as a client-side offline and local multiplayer game. The following categories of data are processed solely on your device:

  • Gameplay Preferences & Settings: Audio toggle states (sound effects on/off), fast move animation toggles, and tactile vibration (haptics) preferences.
  • Game State & Session Progress: Board state, active player turns, pawn coordinates, roll histories, and saved matches to allow players to resume an unfinished session.
  • Statistics & Virtual Bounty Coins: Total games played, games won, win rates, and fictional in-game "Bounty Coins" balance accumulated through gameplay achievements.
  • Non-Personal Technical Diagnostics: Standard non-identifying operating system metrics (e.g., screen density, operating system version, and basic performance metrics) processed automatically by your device's runtime to render animations smoothly.

All of the aforementioned items are stored locally in your device's isolated storage sandbox (SharedPreferences on Android or local web browser storage). None of this data is transmitted or sold to any data brokers.

03 Virtual Currency Disclaimer (No Real-Money Gambling)

CRITICAL NOTICE — PURELY VIRTUAL ENTERTAINMENT:

"Bounty Coins" and any in-game rewards inside Ludo Bounty are strictly fictional virtual game elements designed exclusively for game progression, celebratory feedback, and entertainment purposes.

To avoid any ambiguity and to adhere strictly with Google Play's Gambling and Financial Services policies:

  1. No Real-World Monetary Value: Bounty Coins have no monetary equivalence in real currency, legal tender, gift cards, or physical goods.
  2. No Cash Withdrawals or Payouts: Players cannot withdraw, redeem, transfer, sell, or exchange Bounty Coins for real money under any circumstances.
  3. No Wagering or Betting: Ludo Bounty is not a casino, gambling platform, sports betting app, or paid contest. Winning or losing matches does not involve real-money stakes.

04 How We Use Processed Data

We process local game information exclusively for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To maintain and remember your gameplay preferences (sound effects, haptic vibration, animation speed).
  • To enable the "Continue Last Game" feature so an ongoing match is not lost if the app is closed.
  • To calculate and display local achievements, win counters, and leaderboard statistics.
  • To guarantee optimal board responsiveness, frame rate stability, and touch interaction on various screen sizes.

We do not perform user profiling, behavioral tracking, cross-app tracking, or targeted advertising.

05 Device Permissions Explained

When running on Android devices, Ludo Bounty may request or utilize minimal hardware permissions required strictly for core gameplay:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to trigger subtle haptic vibrations during dice rolls, token captures, and token home runs when haptics are enabled in Settings. Can be toggled off at any time in the in-game Settings.
  • android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Standard network connectivity permissions required for loading web assets, verifying network availability for future online features, and system-level app integrity checks.

The app does NOT request sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, SMS, Phone State, or External Storage files.

07 Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Family Friendly & Safe for All Ages: Ludo Bounty is a traditional board game intended for general audiences, families, and players of all ages.

We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable European jurisdictions) in strict compliance with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Google Play Families Policy.

Because the game operates locally without user accounts or personal profiles, no child's personal identity is exposed or collected. If a parent or guardian believes their child has somehow submitted personal information to us, please reach out to muhammedsadiq54545@gmail.com and we will immediately take steps to permanently delete any such data.

08 Data Retention, Deletion, & Player Rights

Because all game state and settings reside exclusively on your physical device, you maintain full control over your data at all times:

  • Reset In-Game Data: You can reset all accumulated Bounty Coins, match histories, and statistics at any time by opening the in-game Settings modal and tapping "Reset Statistics".
  • Device Clear Storage: On Android devices, you can completely erase all local data by navigating to:
    Device Settings > Apps > Ludo Bounty > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application immediately deletes all locally saved preferences and application files from your device.
  • Data Inquiries & Deletion Requests: If you have any inquiries regarding data handling or wish to submit a formal deletion request, you can email us directly at muhammedsadiq54545@gmail.com.

09 Data Security

We are dedicated to safeguarding your game data. Because Ludo Bounty does not maintain remote database records of your personal credentials or transactions, your exposure to remote database breaches is minimized. Any local data written to your device is protected by the Android sandbox operating system security model, preventing unauthorized access by other applications.
